Manuscript in preparation named after the fable of the blind men and the elphant.
It proposes that AD and most age related conditions are the result of
impaired stomach function and chemistry. Existing disparate theories
are considered in light of this hypothesis and they appear to make
a lot of sense explained this way. An approach to restoring nutrient uptake
and minimizing endogenous toxin production is discussed.
Interestingly, apparently Natalie Merchant set the fable to music
and the name "10,000 maniacs" of course goes well with the topic.
